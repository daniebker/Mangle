Mangle CYD Flasher

Install the Mangle manga reader on a 3.5" ESP32 display board (ESP32-3248S035 "Cheap Yellow Display" or Freenove ESP32-S3 Display) straight from your browser.

1

Choose a release

2

Choose your board

The model is printed on the back of the board. Not sure? The installer reports the chip: ESP32-3248S035 boards have an ESP32, the Freenove board an ESP32-S3.

3

Connect and install

Plug the board in with a USB data cable, then pick its serial port.

Your browser can't talk to USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Installing needs a secure (https) page.

No release has firmware yet. Builds appear here after a version tag is pushed.

After installing
  1. Convert books on your computer: python prep.py book.cbz -o book.zip --device cyd
  2. Copy them into a manga folder on a FAT32 microSD card, and put the card in the board.
  3. The resistive board asks you to press three crosses to calibrate the touch screen. Hold the screen while powering on to do it again.
If it doesn't connect
  • No port in the list: the ESP32-3248S035 boards use a CH340 USB chip, which needs a driver on Windows and older macOS. Some USB cables only carry power; try another.
  • Stuck on "Connecting": hold the BOOT button on the board while you click Connect, and let go once installing starts.
  • Picture looks like a negative after installing: turn on Invert panel in the reader's Settings.
Installing uses ESP Web Tools; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
